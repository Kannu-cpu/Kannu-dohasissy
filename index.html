Ila ukhti, min umm thanya

Aku shi ilich

Dagg 3ala el-zir w istanni shwaya

Rasala ila ukhti el-3azeeza

Shlonich ya ukhti w habibti?

Aku cham kilma ariid aguulha ilich min galbi:

Inti 7elwa, ghalya, w galbich abyad mithl el-thalij.

Mu chidh ma da tfakkirin, inti a7la bkathir.

La tz3alin w la tigsin 3ala nafsich.

Law kunna ikhwa b el-3iraq, chan kil yom nishrab chai w nidh7ak sawa 3ala ay shi.

Ana wiyyaich dayman, bil fara7 w bil ze3al.

Ahebich hwaya ya 3umri, w inti ukhti min umm thanya.

Ukhtich, min galbi
